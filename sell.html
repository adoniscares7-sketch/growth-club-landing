Honest questions.

Will I get full market value?
No — and nobody buying cash will offer that. You're trading some equity for speed, certainty, and zero hassle. If you have a turnkey house in a hot ZIP, listing with an agent gets you more money. If your house needs $20k in repairs or you just want to be done, our offer usually nets you the same or more once you subtract repairs + agent fees + 90 days of holding costs.
Where do you buy?
Atlanta-metro: inside the perimeter + Cobb, Fulton, DeKalb, Gwinnett, Cherokee, Henry, Clayton, Forsyth. Outside those? Tell us where — we may JV with a partner.
Behind on payments? Foreclosure? Probate?
Yes — these are exactly the situations we handle every week. We work with the lender, the court, the heirs. You don't have to figure it out alone.
What's the catch?
No catch on the seller side. We make money on the buyer side — selling the house to a renovator or investor. You get certainty + speed; we take the renovation risk. If our number isn't worth your time, you walk away — no obligation, no pressure call.
