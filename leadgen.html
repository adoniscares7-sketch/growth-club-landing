Questions contractors ask.

Are these exclusive or shared with 5 other contractors?
Exclusive. One contractor per vertical per ZIP. We track every delivery and never sell the same lead to two operators. The whole model breaks otherwise — referrals dry up, refunds stack up, we go out of business. Exclusivity is the business.
Where do these leads come from?
Combination of: Atlanta-metro homeowner forms (we run them), Google reviews + GMB chat capture, paid social funnels for roofing/HVAC, and a referral engine with property managers + realtors. All inbound, all consented, all warm.
What if a lead I get is garbage?
Tell us within 48 hours. If we agree (visible criteria: wrong service area, bot/disconnected, already had service done), we credit your next billing cycle. We track lead quality rolling — your dashboard shows accept/reject so the math is honest.
Why so cheap?
Atlanta-only co-op model. We don't sell nationally. We don't run a sales team. The platform is built on top of GHL + n8n + AI receptionists. Margin per lead is small, but we make it up on volume + retention. Your competitor's national lead service charges $50-$200 per lead — we deliver 3 for $99 because we're not paying a sales team to chase 50 states.
When do I get my first lead?
Average is 4-12 days from signup, depending on vertical + ZIP. Roofing in spring = fast (2-7 days). Pest control in November = slower (10-14 days). If month 1 is zero, you don't pay month 1 — period.
